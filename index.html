THE BIG PICTURE 02 — 12

What is information security?

Information security is the practice of protecting information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.

It protects information wherever it lives — on a device, in a database, on paper, or moving across a network.

↗The goal: make information available to the right people, in the right way, at the right time.

KNOW THE RISKS 04 — 12

The threat landscape.

A threat is anything that can exploit a weakness and harm data, systems, or people.

Threats may come from criminals, careless mistakes, technical failures, or unexpected events. Knowing the risk helps us prepare before an incident happens.

◎Think in layers: prevent, detect, respond, and recover.

KEEP SECRETS SECRET 08 — 12

Encryption & data privacy.

Encryption transforms readable information into protected data that requires the correct key to read.

It helps protect data in transit and at rest. Privacy complements security by guiding how personal information is collected, used, shared, and retained.

⌑Remember: encryption helps protect data, but it does not make every system or user action safe.

RESILIENCE BY DESIGN 09 — 12

Backups & recovery.

Security is not only about preventing incidents. It is also about recovering when something goes wrong.

Maintain tested backups, keep a recovery plan, and store a protected copy that attackers or device failures cannot easily reach.
